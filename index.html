Genetic Evolution

About This Simulation

Watch as red and blue creatures evolve through natural selection. Each creature has unique abilities that can be passed to offspring with potential mutations.

Abilities

  • ⚡ Speed: Move faster to catch prey or escape predators
  • 💪 Strength: Deal more damage in combat
  • 🔭 Range: Detect enemies and allies from further away
  • 🛡️ Shield: Reduce damage taken from attacks
  • ❤️ Heal: Restore health to nearby allies
  • 🧬 Reproduce: More efficiently create offspring

Interaction

Click or tap anywhere to boost nearby creatures of the team that's falling behind, helping maintain evolutionary balance.
